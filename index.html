Settings & sync

Sync to your Dropbox to keep a backup and use the app on more than one device. The app can only see its own folder, Dropbox/Apps/…/calories.json.

In Dropbox, tap Allow, then copy the code it shows and come back here.

Advanced: use your own Dropbox app

Create an app at dropbox.com/developers/apps (Scoped access → App folder), enable files.content.read and files.content.write under Permissions, and paste its App key here. Leave empty to use this app's built-in one.

Connected to Dropbox. Data syncs automatically to Apps/<your app>/calories.json.
